PATIENT CLINICAL NOTE
=====================
Date: 2024-03-15
Patient ID: PT-001
Age: 52 | Gender: Male

CHIEF COMPLAINT:
Patient presents with excessive thirst, frequent urination, and persistent fatigue
lasting approximately 3 months.

The patient reports polyuria and polydipsia with urination occurring up to 10 times
per day including nocturia. He describes extreme fatigue that interferes with daily
activities. He has noticed unexplained weight loss of approximately 8 kg over the
past 2 months despite normal appetite.

SYMPTOMS REPORTED:

- Frequent urination (polyuria)
- Increased thirst (polydipsia)
- Unexplained weight loss
- Blurred vision (intermittent)
- Tingling and numbness in feet

- Fatigue and weakness
- Dry mouth

MEDICATIONS:
None currently. Patient has not sought treatment previously.

FAMILY HISTORY:
Father diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes at age 48.
Mother has hypertension.

ASSESSMENT:
Strong clinical presentation consistent with uncontrolled Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus.
HbA1c and fasting glucose levels to be confirmed via lab work.
Insulin resistance suspected based on symptom profile and family history.

PLAN:

- HbA1c measurement
- Initiate metformin pending lab confirmation
- Dietary counseling referral
- Ophthalmology referral for blurred vision
